About PULSE

Built by players, run like a league.

Founded in 2018 by a group of former competitive players, PULSE started as a single Counter-Strike roster practicing out of a rented apartment. Today we operate professional teams across five titles, a broadcast studio, and a tournament circuit that pays out real prize money to grassroots talent.

We're not a brand chasing esports — we're a team that grew into one, and every decision still gets made with a controller or mouse in hand.

  • Full-time coaching & analyst staff
  • In-house broadcast & production studio
  • Open qualifiers for every circuit event
  • Player wellness & performance program
